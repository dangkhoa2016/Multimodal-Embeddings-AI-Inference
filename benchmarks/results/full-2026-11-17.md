## Provenance and limitations

All 65 recorded POST attempts completed and were confirmed. The independent offline audit recomputes the summary and checks file digests. The Git commit is operator-provided and is corroborated by Modal deployment history; it is not a cryptographic attestation of GPU runtime binaries. Cold client latency includes submission, queue/provisioning, loading, inference, polling, and network. GPU provisioning delay is not independently measured. Idle time does not independently prove worker scale-down. Small-sample p95 is not a production SLA, retrieval quality, or face-recognition accuracy measure.
