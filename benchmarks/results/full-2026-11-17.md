## Provenance and limitations

All 65 recorded POST attempts completed and were confirmed. The independent offline audit recomputes the summary and checks file digests. The deployment source identifier was operator-provided and corroborated by Modal deployment history. The public provenance JSON retains recorded source and measurement metadata but redacts the direct operator deployment URL; the unchanged original is kept separately in a private operator archive. The historical deployment source snapshot is not distributed; these records do not constitute cryptographic attestation of GPU runtime binaries. Cold client latency includes submission, queue/provisioning, loading, inference, polling, and network. GPU provisioning delay is not independently measured. Idle time does not independently prove worker scale-down. Small-sample p95 is not a production SLA, retrieval quality, or face-recognition accuracy measure.
