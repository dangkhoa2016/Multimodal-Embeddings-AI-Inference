## Limitations and provenance

- Small samples (Cold n=5/model, Warm n=4/modality/model, Alternating n=5/model, Concurrent n=4/model, Mixed Batch n=3) make p95 descriptive only, not a production tail-latency promise.
- Cold client time includes dispatch, provisioning/queue, GPU model load, execution, polling and network. The job API does not separately expose GPU allocation waiting; its cause cannot be isolated from the total.
- These measurements validate selected inference requests, GPU telemetry, routing and response identity; they do not establish retrieval quality, face-identity accuracy, sustained load or production SLA.
- In the **original** JSON, `client_source_commit` and `server_source_commit` are `null`. An embedded notebook-template digest is **not independently runtime-attested**. This report does not retroactively assign or verify a server source commit.
- The [17 November 2026 benchmark](full-2026-11-17.md) is retained as an independent earlier run. Do not mix its trials with this continuous session.
